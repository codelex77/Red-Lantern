Our Story

A Taste of Beijing
in Botswana

Nestled in Broadhurst, Gaborone, Red Lantern has been a cornerstone of authentic Chinese dining in Botswana for over two decades. Since opening our doors, we have brought the rich culinary heritage of Beijing — its bold flavours, time-honoured recipes, and warm hospitality — to the heart of Southern Africa.

From our signature sizzling pork dishes and hand-prepared dumplings to freshly wok-tossed noodles and whole-steamed fish, every dish is crafted with care by our experienced kitchen team. Whether you join us for a relaxed weekday lunch or an intimate VIP dinner, the Red Lantern experience is one you will not forget.
